### About me

I build business software end to end — from the database schema to the screen on a shop counter.
These days I mostly work in **TypeScript (NestJS + Next.js)** on multi-tenant SaaS platforms,
and I have shipped ERP, CRM, POS, healthcare and pharmacy systems used by real businesses every day.

- 🏢 Full-stack developer at **Pentasoft Yazılım**, working on the *Puma* software family for bakeries and pastry shops
- 🚀 Building a multi-tenant, all-in-one business management platform (ERP · CRM · operations)
- 🔌 Comfortable where software meets hardware: POS terminals, fiscal cash registers, barcode scanners and scales
- 🤖 Into practical automation: background jobs with queues, Playwright and n8n
- 🌍 Open to remote and international full-stack roles
