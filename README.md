### 👨‍💻 About me

I have been writing software since I was 13 and building products professionally since 2012. I started with C#, then C and Java, and for years now I have been focused on the JavaScript / TypeScript ecosystem.

Today I mostly build **multi-tenant SaaS and business software** with **NestJS, Next.js, Prisma, PostgreSQL, Redis and BullMQ**: ERP, CRM, POS, healthcare and pharmacy systems used by real businesses every day. I also enjoy the places where software meets hardware: POS terminals, fiscal cash registers, barcode scanners and scales.

- 🌍 Based in **İzmir, Türkiye**
- 🏢 Full-stack developer at **Pentasoft Yazılım**, working on the *Puma* software family for bakeries & pastry shops
- 🚀 Building a **multi-tenant, all-in-one business management platform** (ERP · CRM · operations)
- 🧠 Learning: AI-assisted development, automation (Playwright, n8n) and scalable SaaS architecture
- 🤝 Open to SaaS, ERP / CRM and retail / POS projects, and to remote full-stack roles
- ⚡ Hello, entrepreneur!
